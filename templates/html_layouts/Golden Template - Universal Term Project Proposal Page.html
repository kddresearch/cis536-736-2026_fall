⚠️ Protocol: Individual Proposals, Team Projects

To ensure every student has a grasp of the project scope, we follow a strict submission protocol:

  • Proposals are INDIVIDUAL: Even if you have formed a team, EACH member must submit their own PDF proposal.
  • What You Can Share: Team members may copy/paste the same Problem Statement and Background/Related Work sections.
  • What Must Be Unique: Your Methodology and Milestones must reflect your specific role and contributions to the effort.
  • Co-Authoring: You may submit shared Presentations and Final Reports ONLY AFTER your project has been approved and you have been assigned an official Team Number.

Methodological Pillars

This course engages the five pillars of modern AI—language, vision, learning, trust, and systems—to transition students from theoretical understanding to the construction of robust, intelligent agents.

Natural Language [NL]

Focuses on representation, extraction, reasoning, trust, and longitudinal modeling of meaning in text and multimodal language artifacts.

Computer Vision [CV]

Focuses on structured perception, hierarchical interpretation, and explainable visual understanding, especially where vision is tightly coupled with language.

Reinforcement Learning [RL]

Focuses on learning, reasoning, and decision-making under uncertainty, emphasizing interpretability, causal structure, and semantic representations.

Secure & Trustworthy [ST]

Focuses on ensuring reliability, robustness, interpretability, and alignment of learning and reasoning systems in high-stakes environments.

Applied AI Systems [OA]

Serves as the translational and integrative layer connecting foundational research to real-world systems, constraints, and performance considerations.

Project Category Tracks

Your project generally falls into one of these four categories.

Track 1: Search, Games, and Planning

This category covers problems where an agent must navigate a state space to achieve a goal, often against an adversary or within complex constraints. While classical search algorithms (A*, Minimax) are foundational, modern projects often integrate Reinforcement Learning [RL] to handle high-dimensional state spaces.

Track 2: Knowledge, Logic, and Reasoning

This category focuses on representing the world through symbols, ontologies, and logical rules. Projects here often utilize Secure & Trustworthy [ST] methodologies to ensure that reasoning systems are not only correct but transparent and free from logical fallacies or biases.

Track 3: Machine Learning & Probabilistic Models

This category encompasses statistical modeling, spanning from Bayesian Networks to Deep Learning. A critical aspect is often the Applied AI Systems [OA] methodology, bridging the gap between theoretical models and usable tools.

Track 4: Perception and Agentic Systems

This category deals with agents that perceive their environment through sensors (text, vision, audio). This track heavily utilizes Computer Vision [CV] and Natural Language [NL] to ground abstract data into actionable information.

Proposal Document Format

Your proposal must be a 1–1.5 page PDF containing the following five sections:

1. Introduction

  • Problem Statement: Clearly describe the problem you intend to solve.
  • Goal: What will your system accomplish?
  • Context: Define your dataset/use case. Is this Research (novelty) or Application (utility)?

2. Background & Related Work

  • Summarize relevant prior work.
  • Requirement: At least 3 references.
  • Requirement: At least one archival/peer-reviewed source (ACM, IEEE, etc.).

3. Methodology

  • Baselines: What existing method will serve as comparison? (e.g., "Random Forest").
  • Proposed Approach: What new method/architecture will you implement?
  • Specifics: Detail your architecture, algorithms, and libraries. Avoid vague statements.

4. Evaluation Plan

  • Quantitative: Accuracy, F1, MSE, Runtime.
  • Qualitative: Visual fidelity, user study.
  • Validation: Train/test splits, cross-validation, unit testing.

5. Milestones & Timeline

  • List 3–6 dated objectives (e.g., "{{DATE_MILESTONE_EX}}: Baseline Implemented").
