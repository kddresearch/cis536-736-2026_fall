Welcome to CIS 531/731 and CIS 536/736!

## 1. Viewing the Public Calendar
My public calendar displays **only** Free/Busy blocks. It does not expose the titles, attendees, or details of occupied meetings.

* 📅 **View My Live Availability Here:** [Dr. Hsu's Outlook Calendar](https://outlook.cloud.microsoft/calendar/published/736e127f3a50453a84ec1aa133e5771f@ksu.edu/c3b12ed536e34ce18abf032c0a37e1de6388063591801811409/calendar.html)

**How to Read This Calendar:**
* The calendar defaults to a Month or Week view. Use the arrows at the top left to navigate to the current week.
* Solid colored blocks labeled "Busy" indicate I am unavailable. 
* You are looking for **blank white space** during normal business hours. That is when you may request a meeting!

*(Please note: I am generally unavailable during my MWF teaching blocks from 10:30-11:20 AM and 12:30-1:20 PM, as well as Monday afternoons from 2:30-5:30 PM).*

## 2. Booking an Appointment
To place an appointment on my calendar, please use the Calendly portal. The portal is synchronized with my live calendar and will only offer times where I am genuinely available. 

* 🤝 **Schedule an Appointment via Calendly:** [https://calendly.com/banazir/](https://calendly.com/banazir/)

### Setting Up Our Meeting for Success
To make sure we can dive right into solving your problem when we meet, I ask that you provide a little bit of context upfront. 

When filling out the booking form on Calendly, please include:
1. Your Name and Course (e.g., CIS 536).
2. A brief note on what you'd like to discuss.
3. **A link to the artifact** you want me to look at in advance (e.g., a link to your GitHub Issue, a committed `.md` file, or your code repository). 

*If you want to discuss your artifact in an email before or after our meeting, please message me through the Canvas inbox. Always include the link to your artifact and mention the date and time of any appointment you booked. Using Canvas ensures your message goes to my priority inbox for the quickest reply!*
