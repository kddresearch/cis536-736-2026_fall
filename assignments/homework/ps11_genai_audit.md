# PS 11: GenAI Audit & Process Reflection
**Auditing Your Semester's GenAI Usage and the Open-Weight vs. Proprietary Tradeoff**

**🗓️ Released:** Fri 27 Nov 2026 | **📅 Due:** Sun 06 Dec 2026 | **💯 Points:** 100 | **👥 Team:** Individual

> **🚨 CRITICAL SUBMISSION POLICY**
> **ℹ️ This Assignment Documents Process, Not Just a Single Artifact:** Unlike other assignments, PS 11 asks you to look back across your entire semester's GenAI Kit usage (HW 1 through MP 10) and forward into your Term Project's GenAI Audit section — it is explicitly reflective, not a new technical build.

## 1. Required Readings
* **Course GenAI Policy (Syllabus):**
  * **GenAI Audit Requirements:** What must be disclosed and how.
  * **Proprietary vs. Open-Weight Rules:** What's forbidden for core work vs. permitted for the Term Project.
* **Shap-E / Point-E / gsplat Licensing Pages:**
  * **Open-Weight License Terms:** For grounding your Part 2 reflection in fact, not impression.

## 2. Part 1: Semester GenAI Usage Audit
*(45 Points)*

Review your own submitted `GenAI.pdf` logs (or "No GenAI Used" statements) from HW 1 through MP 10.

**Audit Summary:**
* Summarize, across all prior assignments, which stages of the AI Kit procedure you actually used versus skipped, and whether that changed over the semester as your own skills grew.
* Identify one assignment where GenAI assistance meaningfully changed your approach (for better or worse), and one where you deliberately chose not to use it and why.

## 3. Part 2: Open-Weight vs. Proprietary Tradeoff Reflection
*(50 Points)*

**Objective:** Connect your semester's hands-on experience with open-weight tools (Shap-E, Point-E, gsplat) to the course's generative-constraints invariant.

1. Compare your experience quality/friction with the required open-weight tools in MP 7 against what you understand (from documentation, not necessarily hands-on use) about equivalent proprietary alternatives.
2. State whether you used a proprietary generative API for optional Term Project exploration (per the GenAI Policy); if so, document it here as your formal audit entry.
3. Argue, from your own experience rather than the syllabus's stated rationale, whether the open-weight-only requirement for core assignments was pedagogically justified.

**Deliverable:** A single PDF reflection; this is a writing assignment, no code or GitHub commit is required.

## 4. Grading Rubric (100 Points Total)

| Component | Weight | Description |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Audit Completeness & Honesty** | 25 | Covers the full HW 1–MP 10 span candidly, not selectively. |
| **Specific Assignment Examples** | 20 | The "changed my approach" and "chose not to use it" examples are concrete, not generic. |
| **Open-Weight/Proprietary Comparison Depth** | 30 | Comparison is grounded in actual MP 7 experience, not just restating the syllabus. |
| **Term Project GenAI Disclosure** | 20 | Any proprietary API use on the Term Project is disclosed here, matching what appears in the Term Project's own GenAI Audit section. |
| **GenAI Audit Compliance** | 5 | Yes, this assignment is itself subject to the same policy — required log/statement included. |
