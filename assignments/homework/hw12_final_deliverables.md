# HW 12: Final Project Deliverables
**Final OpenUSD-Composed Scene Package and Term Project Report**

**🗓️ Released:** Sat 05 Dec 2026 | **📅 Due:** Mon 14 Dec 2026 | **💯 Points:** 100 | **👥 Team:** Individual

> **🚨 CRITICAL SUBMISSION POLICY**
> **ℹ️ Mandatory Milestone — Due First Day of Finals Week:** This is the third and final mandatory Term Project milestone and is excluded from the regular-assignment drop policy. It is due Mon 14 Dec — the first day of finals week, immediately after Project Presentations (Dec 7–11) — via commit to your GitHub Classroom repository.

## 1. Overview
Your Term Project must demonstrate working integration of at least 2 of the 5 course pillars (Procedural Modeling, Rigging/Animation, Generative Geometry/3DGS, Cloud Rendering, Visualization), composed via OpenUSD. You will submit your final technical deliverables alongside a written report documenting what you built, how it integrates, and how it evolved from your PS 4 proposal and PS 8 interim report.

**Required Readings:**
* **OpenUSD.org — Composition Overview:**
  * **Full Recap:** References, payloads, variant sets — as used in your final package.
* **Course Syllabus — Term Project Rubric & GitHub Classroom Protocol:**
  * **Final Submission Requirements:** What must be in the final commit, and how it's graded.

## 2. Part 1: Final Technical Deliverable
*(50 Points)*

**Technical Requirements:**
* Submit your final, composed OpenUSD stage demonstrating correct composition arcs (references/payloads/variant sets as appropriate) across your chosen pillars.
* Submit your final Unity 6 project/scene with all chosen-pillar assets correctly integrated and functioning as demonstrated in your presentation.

## 3. Part 2: Final Written Report & GenAI Audit
*(45 Points)*

**Objective:** Document what you built, how it integrates, and how it evolved from your PS 4 proposal and PS 8 interim report.

1. Summarize your final pillar integration and how it differs from your original PS 4 proposal (if at all).
2. Include your complete GenAI Audit section, consistent with your PS 11 disclosures, covering the entire Term Project.
3. State one thing you would do differently with another semester's worth of time.

**Deliverable:** A GitHub Classroom repository commit containing your final OpenUSD stage, Unity 6 project, and PDF report; post the absolute commit URL to Canvas.

## 4. Grading Rubric (100 Points Total)

| Component | Weight | Description |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Cross-Pillar Integration Correctness** | 30 | At least 2 pillars genuinely integrate via OpenUSD, not merely coexist in the same scene. |
| **OpenUSD Composition Quality** | 20 | Composition arcs are used correctly and the stage opens cleanly. |
| **Final Report Completeness & Honesty** | 25 | Report accurately reflects what was built, including gaps from the original proposal. |
| **GenAI Audit Completeness** | 15 | Full-project GenAI Audit is present and consistent with PS 11. |
| **Submission Protocol Compliance** | 10 | Correct commit URL posted to Canvas by the deadline, per GitOps protocol. |
