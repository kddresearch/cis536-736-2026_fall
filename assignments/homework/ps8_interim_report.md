## 1. Overview
Submit your Term Project progress checkpoint to report on your preliminary cross-pillar integration results. This report serves to verify that you are actively implementing the ≥2-pillar OpenUSD-interchange requirement you outlined in your PS 4 proposal.

**Required Readings:**
* **Course GenAI & Term Project Policy (Syllabus):**
  * **Term Project Scope Section:** The ≥2-pillar OpenUSD-interchange requirement you are checking your progress against.
  * **Milestone Rubric Overview:** How PS 4, PS 8, and HW 12 relate to each other.
* **OpenUSD.org — Composition Overview:**
  * **Composition Arcs Recap:** References, payloads, and variant sets, as used in your interim integration.

## 2. Initiating Your Draft (The AI Kit Procedure)
Use the Generative AI Kit to help structure your submission. Follow these stages to systematically break down your assignment:

1. **Narrative Outlining:** Ask the AI Kit to help structure your 400–600 word progress narrative by summarizing your PS 4 goals and your current reality.
2. **Risk Assessment:** Discuss your biggest remaining technical roadblocks with the AI Kit to brainstorm and formally document mitigation strategies.
3. **Architecture Diagramming:** Have the AI Kit draft a text-based hierarchy or graph representing your current OpenUSD stage's composition arcs based on your descriptions.
4. **Schedule Generation:** Work with the AI Kit to break down your remaining integration gaps into a week-by-week sprint schedule leading up to HW 12.
5. **Report Assembly:** Synthesize the text outputs into your final PDF, ensuring placeholders for your screenshot and GitHub URL are clearly marked for manual inclusion.

## 3. Required Document Structure
Your submission must be a **PDF report to Canvas + GitHub Classroom commit URL (interim state)** containing the following sections exactly as named below. 

* **Part 1: Progress Narrative:** A 400–600 word status update on your Term Project against your PS 4 proposal. Explicitly state which 2+ pillars (Procedural, Rigging/Animation, Generative/3DGS, Cloud Rendering, Visualization) you are integrating and confirm if this matches your approved proposal.
* **Part 2: Risk Assessment:** Identify your single biggest technical risk remaining before the Final Deliverable and detail your plan for addressing it.
* **Part 3: Integration Evidence:** Include a screenshot or short screen capture showing assets from your two pillars composed together in a single OpenUSD-driven Unity 6 scene.
* **Part 4: OpenUSD Sublayer Structure:** A simple diagram or text hierarchy mapping your current OpenUSD stage's sublayer and composition-arc structure.
* **Part 5: Remaining Schedule:** List what remains unfinished and your remaining-weeks schedule to close the gap before HW 12.
* **GenAI Audit:** Did you use the Generative AI Kit to synthesize this artifact? You must include your `GenAI.pdf` log or explicitly state "No GenAI Used."

## 4. Grading Rubric (100 Points Total)

This assignment is worth 100 points total.

**Part 1: Progress Narrative (40 pts)**
* **Progress Narrative Honesty & Clarity (20 pts):** Status is reported candidly, including risks, not just successes.
* **Scope Alignment with Proposal (20 pts):** Pillar selection is confirmed, or any divergence is justified.

**Part 2: Preliminary Integration Evidence (55 pts)**
* **Integration Evidence Quality (35 pts):** Screenshot/capture genuinely shows two pillars composed via OpenUSD, not two disconnected scenes.
* **Remaining-Work Plan Specificity (20 pts):** The plan to close remaining gaps is concrete and time-bound.
