### ⚠️ Grading & Late Work Policies
* **Constraint Notice:** This schedule guarantees a minimum 9-calendar-day lead time from release to due date for all assignments. It also enforces a strict minimum 6-day gap between assignment due dates to protect student workload. The *sole exception* is the 4-day gap between PS 4 and MP 5, which is an explicit exemption resulting from the formal extension of PS 4 to Oct 14.
* **Drop Policy:** The highest **7 of your 9 regular assignments** (non-project HWs and MPs) will count toward your final grade. The lowest 2 scores are dropped automatically.
* **Mandatory Milestones:** The Term Project Milestones (PS 4, PS 8, HW 12) are explicitly marked below. These are mandatory and **cannot be dropped**.
* **Zero Late Work Allowed:** Late submissions are not accepted. If an assignment closes, you will receive zero points unless you have a pre-approved, documented OSSA emergency.
